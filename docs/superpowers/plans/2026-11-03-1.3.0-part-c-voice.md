# Timerify 1.3.0 Part C (voice: clock phrases) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Assist's built-in agent understands more ways of saying a clock time (#49): minutes past or to an hour in English ("half past 7", "quarter to 8", "twenty-five to 8"), the Russian count to the next hour and "bez", a part of the day said before the time ("tonight at 7", "this morning at 7", the Russian "zavtra utrom v 7"), and "7:30am" written together.

**Architecture:**
- **Words in `sentences/<lang>/src/` only.** Each list value is one word with its own number ("half" 30, "quarter" 15, an ordinal hour 1 to 12). The direction is the slot a template fills: `clock_minute` for minutes after the hour (as today), the new `clock_before` for minutes before it, and the new `clock_ordinal` for the Russian "Nth hour".
- **One Python function does the arithmetic.** `ClockCommandHandler.when` turns an ordinal into its hour (N-1, with 0 read as 12) and "tonight" into the night of today. `clock_moment(..., before=)` takes the minutes off on the wall clock, after the day and the part of the day are read. Nothing in Python knows a word of any language.
- **The generators do not change.** `scripts/` is untouched, and `core_commands.yaml` comes out byte-identical. A third language would need only its files in `sentences/<lang>/src/`.

**Tech Stack:** Python 3.14, Home Assistant 2026.10.4, hassil 3.12.1, home-assistant-intents 2026.10.6, pytest with freezegun, unicode_rbnf 2.4.1 (already installed with hassil, used by one test).

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-03-release-1.3.0-design.md`, section "Part C: voice", plus "Release", "Non-goals" and "Constraints". Owner's decisions: `.superpowers/issue-triage-1.3.0.md`. Issue: `gh issue view 49` (and `gh issue view 21` for the comment at the end).

**Research:** `.superpowers/research-1.3.0-voice.md`. Section 6 is the design and section 4 the Russian grammar. ### Task 1: The arithmetic of minutes around an hour and of "tonight"

**Files:**
- Modify: `custom_components/timerify/voice/commands.py` (`DayPart`, `hour_of_day`, `clock_moment`, `ClockCommandHandler`: docstring, `description`, `slot_schema`, `when`), `RULINGS.md`.
- Test: `tests/voice/test_clock_commands.py`, section "The rules the handler reads", and the handler-level tests beside `test_a_time_with_no_hour_that_is_not_noon_or_midnight_is_not_understood`.

**Interfaces:**
- Produces:
  - `DayPart` gains `"tonight"`. `hour_of_day(hour, "tonight")` is the same as for `"night"` (the match stays total).
  - `clock_moment(..., before: int = 0)`. Each candidate is the wall time `h:minute` on its day, minus `before` minutes as naive wall arithmetic, then `wall_moments(date, time, zone)`. With no day word the days searched are 0, 1 and 2 (the third is for "quarter to midnight" said after 23:45). With `before=0` every result is the same as today.
  - `ClockCommandHandler.slot_schema` gains `probatio.Optional("clock_before"): All(whole_slot, Range(min=1, max=59))` and `probatio.Optional("clock_ordinal"): All(whole_slot, Range(min=1, max=12))`. `clock_part` accepts `"tonight"`.
  - `when` reads them in this order:
    1. `"tonight"` becomes `"night"`, with `day = 0` when no day is given;
    2. an ordinal given with `clock_hour` or `clock_before` raises `probatio.Invalid`;
    3. so does `clock_before` given with `clock_minute`;
    4. an ordinal becomes `hour = ordinal - 1 or _HALF_DAY`;
    5. the rest is as today, and `before` is passed to every `clock_moment` call.
  - Noon and midnight with `clock_before` stay valid (hour None, part `am` or `pm`, no minute).

```yaml
  timerify_part_hour: "(<existing alternatives>|<timerify_clock_relative_hour>)"
  timerify_exact_time: "(<existing alternatives>|<timerify_clock_relative>)"
  timerify_clock_relative: "(<timerify_clock_relative_hour> [{timerify_day_parts:clock_part}]|<timerify_clock_before_amount> to {timerify_clock_words:clock_part})"
  timerify_clock_relative_hour: "(<timerify_clock_after_amount> past {timerify_half_day_hours:clock_hour}|<timerify_clock_before_amount> to {timerify_half_day_hours:clock_hour})"
  timerify_clock_after_amount: "({timerify_minutes_of_hour:clock_minute} [minute[s]]|{timerify_clock_fractions:clock_minute})"
  timerify_clock_before_amount: "({timerify_minutes_of_hour:clock_before} [minute[s]]|{timerify_clock_fractions_before:clock_before})"
```

English lists: `timerify_clock_fractions` holds `"half"` 30 and `"[a] quarter"` 15. `timerify_clock_fractions_before` holds `"[a] quarter"` 15 only, so "half to 8" is not understood.

Russian `src`. The words are transliterated here, and the source has them in Cyrillic:
- "v" is the preposition, spelt as in the current `timerify_clock_time`;
- "bez" is the preposition "without";
- "minut[a|u|y]" are the forms of "minuta", spelt as the current "minut[u|y]" is.

`timerify_clock_time` keeps its current alternative "v (...)" whole, and gains two:

```yaml
  timerify_clock_time: "(v (<the current alternatives>)|[v] <timerify_clock_into> [{timerify_day_parts:clock_part}]|<timerify_clock_before> [{timerify_day_parts:clock_part}])"
  timerify_clock_into: "({timerify_minutes_of_hour:clock_minute} minut[a|u|y] <timerify_clock_ordinal>|{timerify_clock_fractions:clock_minute}[ ]<timerify_clock_ordinal>)"
  timerify_clock_ordinal: "{timerify_clock_hour_ordinals:clock_ordinal}"
  timerify_clock_before: "bez ({timerify_minutes_of_hour:clock_before} [minut[y]]|{timerify_clock_fractions_before:clock_before}) ({timerify_half_day_hours:clock_hour}|{timerify_clock_hour_one:clock_hour})"
```

Russian lists, in Cyrillic in the source:
- `timerify_clock_fractions`: `(polovina|polovine|polovinu|pol)` 30, `chetvert'` 15;
- `timerify_clock_fractions_before`: `chetverti` 15;
- `timerify_clock_hour_ordinals`: the genitive masculine ordinals pervogo 1, vtorogo 2, tret'ego 3, `(chetvyortogo|chetvertogo)` 4, pyatogo 5, shestogo 6, sed'mogo 7, vos'mogo 8, devyatogo 9, desyatogo 10, odinnadtsatogo 11, dvenadtsatogo 12;
- `timerify_clock_hour_one`: `chas` 1.

No list of Russian cardinals in the genitive is added: the ranges already match "pyati", "dvadtsati pyati" and "odnoy".

The algorithm of `clock_moment`. A body is given because the signature does not fix it:

```python
if part is not None:
    hours = (hour % 12, hour % 12 + 12)            # a part wins over a zero and over 13-23
elif not zero and 1 <= hour <= 12:
    hours = (hour % 12, hour % 12 + 12)
else:
    hours = (hour,)
# candidates: (wall time with `before` taken off, h) for each h and each date searched
# - a part: the windows of the dates (day - 1, day) when day == 0, (day,) for another day,
#   (-1, 0, 1, 2) with no day word; for a window date w the candidate dates are w and w + 1,
#   and a candidate is kept when start(w) <= wall < start(w) + 12 h (on the wall clock).
# - no part, a day word: dates (day, day + 1), kept when the wall date is `day` (as now).
# - no part, no day word: dates (0, 1, 2), all kept (as now).
# order: by moment, except that with a day word and no part, for the hour 12 said with no
#   zero, the reading after noon (h == 12) comes before the one after midnight (h == 0).
# result: the first in that order that is after `now`; if none is, the first (refused).
```

The DST handling through `wall_moments` stays as it is.
